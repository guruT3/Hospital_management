// FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

What MediQ Does

Every capability below is a documented functional requirement of the system (SRS §3.2), covering registration through to prescription and record management.

Unique Health ID Registration

Patients register and instantly receive a permanent Health ID (MD-2026-XXXX) that unifies their medical history across the system.

Role-Based Secure Login

Separate authenticated portals for Admin, Doctor and Patient, each with credentials scoped to their own role and permissions.

Conflict-Aware Booking

Patients book OPD appointments with a chosen doctor; the system detects and blocks conflicting double-bookings for the same slot.

Emergency Priority Queue

Appointments can be flagged as emergency cases and are automatically prioritized ahead of routine bookings in the doctor's queue.

Smart Digital Queue

Each doctor's patient queue updates live, reducing physical waiting-room time and giving patients a clear sense of their position.

Prescriptions & Allergy Alerts

Doctors create prescriptions directly against a patient's record; the system flags a warning if any prescribed medicine matches a recorded allergy.

Auto PDF Prescriptions

Every confirmed prescription is instantly rendered as a downloadable PDF, generated using the ReportLab library on the backend.

Medical Records & Visit History

Patients upload and download diagnostic reports and medical records, while the system keeps a complete visit history for every Health ID.

Nearby Hospital Locator

Detect your current location or search any area, and instantly see nearby hospitals on a live map with distance, contact details and turn-by-turn directions.

Doctor Feedback & Ratings

After a confirmed visit, patients rate their doctor as Good, Average or Poor, and admins see the feedback summary for every doctor.
