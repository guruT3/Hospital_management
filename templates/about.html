Overview

About the Project

MediQ is a full-stack Hospital Management System built with Flask, HTML, CSS, JavaScript and MySQL. It brings patient registration, doctor management, appointment scheduling, a smart digital queue and prescription handling onto a single, secure web-based platform — reducing hospitals' and clinics' dependency on paper-based processes.

Capabilities

Key Features

  • Patient registration with automatic, unique Health ID generation
  • Role-based login and dashboards for Admin, Doctor and Patient
  • Doctor management and availability tracking
  • Appointment booking with automatic conflict detection
  • Emergency priority scheduling for critical cases
  • Smart Digital Queue System to reduce patient waiting time
  • Medicine allergy alerts while creating prescriptions
  • Automatic PDF prescription generation
  • Medical record and diagnostic report upload/download
  • Complete patient visit history tracking
